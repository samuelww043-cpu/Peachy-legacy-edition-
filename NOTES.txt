GAMEPLAY TESTING

The goal of Peachy Legacy is to reduce RAM usage without changing the actual gameplay, terrain generation, visuals, or game mechanics.

Testing included:

- Seeds
- Spawn points
- Stronghold positions
- Terrain generation
- Visuals
- Mob behavior
- Mob AI
- World output

1,000 mobs were spawned in both the original build and Peachy Legacy to compare mob AI and behavior.

The same seeds were also tested for spawn points and stronghold placement.

World files were compared using their SHA/hash to check that Peachy was producing the same world output as the original build.
